# Menu sound credits

Menu sounds are adapted from **51 UI sound effects (buttons, switches and clicks)** by **Kenney**, released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**.

- [Original sound pack and license](https://opengameart.org/content/51-ui-sound-effects-buttons-switches-and-clicks)
- [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- [Kenney UI Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio)

`menu-click.wav` derives from `switch24.wav`; `menu-hover.wav` derives from `rollover6.wav`. The source timelines are preserved. Playback uses normalized files, reduced volume, slight pitch reduction and a low-pass filter.

Credit is retained voluntarily. No endorsement by the original author is claimed.
